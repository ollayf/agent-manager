Read without restoring

read prints a raw file’s archive location. For compressed data, read --head N streams a sample. Over 10 GB, full decompression requires --full.

Bring one file back

restore checks size, hash, free space and metadata. It removes the record and archive copy, saves the indexes, then repairs the parked folder tree.

Investigate an interruption

verify is read-only. Inspect its findings before verify --fix. Differing copies and unknown orphans remain for a person to inspect.

Release boundaries

Every record carries format and writer version. .history/ freezes authentic 2.3.1 and the complete 3.0.0 runtime. Earlier original releases remain unavailable.
